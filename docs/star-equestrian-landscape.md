# Star Equestrian's landscape, and how ours beats it

Research pass, 2026-09-06. Sources at the bottom.

## What they have

Star Equestrian is set in **Evervale**: "a land of stunning vistas and lush forests", "wild
and untamed forests, bustling towns full of people, and Western outposts", "three living,
breathing towns surrounded by mysterious forests and open plains". Four named competition
regions:

| Region | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Heartside** | the starting town and its meadows |
| **Agricola** | farmland |
| **Bronco** | a western desert south of the Redwood Forest, across a river: rocky canyons, desert foliage, a few oases |
| **Witberg** | the fourth championship region (the name reads as "white mountain") |

Also named: the **Redwood Forest**. Reviewers describe the art as "very cartoonish …
reminiscent of the first renditions of Star Stable Online back in 2013", and note the cross
country courses "do an excellent job of showing off the game's lovely environments". One
reviewer "didn't take long to explore all of the first region".

## What we already have

Our region list is a match or better, and it is one continuous world rather than gated areas:
Home Pasture, Coyote Canyon (their Bronco), Barleyfold Farms (their Agricola), Hollowpeak
Heights and Hollowpeak Pines (their Witberg and Redwood), Cottonwood Village (their
Heartside), Riverside Crossing, Loon Lake, Meadowlark Ranch, Kestrel Basin Meadows. Plus a
real day/night cycle, weather, a carved river with a bridge, Weber-Penn trees and a
travelling meadow of grass — none of which their screenshots show.

## The gap, and the plan

Their world reads richer at ground level and has more landmark spectacle. Backlog:

1. **Ground cover by biome** — ferns and scrub in the meadow, dry brush in the canyon, dead
   tufts through the snow, bulrushes at the water, stones everywhere. *(shipped)*
2. **A waterfall** off Hollowpeak into a plunge pool, with mist and a rainbow in it.
3. **Winding dirt paths** joining the ranch, the village and the farms.
4. **Mesas and hoodoos** in Coyote Canyon so it reads as their Bronco does.
5. **Valley mist at dawn** and god rays through the trees.
6. **Seasonal colour** — a band of gold and red trees, not only green and blossom.
7. **A shoreline that reads**: foam at the waterline, lily pads, wet sand.
