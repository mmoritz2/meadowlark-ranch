# Star Ranch Fable 🐴✨

A cozy open-world 3D horse ranch game inspired by Star Equestrian's gameplay —
built from scratch with original code and procedurally generated art, and with
**no ads, no energy timers, and no paywalls**. Everything is earnable by playing.

## How to play

Open `ranch3d.html` in any browser (or `index.html`, which redirects there).
Your ranch auto-saves in the browser — your horses are always waiting.

- 🏇 **Ride**: W/↑ to go, A/D to steer, Shift to gallop, Space to jump — or use
  the on-screen buttons. Drag to orbit the camera, scroll to zoom, 📷 for photos.
- 🐴 **Care** (top bar): feed, water, groom and pet your horse — needs decay over
  real time (gently, never below 15 offline), and care builds bond ❤️ and XP.
- 🛍️ **Shop**: 10 breeds up to a legendary 🦄 Unicorn, food, leather saddles in
  five colors, and 💞 breeding — foals blend both parents' coats and gambol tiny.
- 🏆 **Events**: 8 show-jumping courses plus 3 cross-country flag-gate races that
  loop out over the bridge, across the river, past the village and back to the
  arena. Trophies, star ratings, prize money, and training XP.
- 🌍 **Evervale**: procedurally generated rolling hills, a carved river with a
  wooden bridge, wheat fields, lavender, boulders, leaf-card forests, wild
  horses to tame, riding companions on the trails, missions, weather with rain
  and rainbows, a drifting sun, music and birdsong.

Free, properly-licensed extras: the stork, flamingo and parrot are MIT-licensed
animated models from the three.js project. Everything else is generated in code.

## Why it's "better"

- No energy bars, ads, or microtransactions — gems come from play
- Nothing bad can ever happen to your horses
- One file, runs in a browser, saves automatically
