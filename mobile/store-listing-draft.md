# Meadowlark Ranch — App Store listing draft

**Development draft; not ready to publish.** This describes the planned offline native edition using features present in the game. Copy and screenshots must be checked against the final tested iOS build. Asset rights, metadata, privacy, signing, and device validation remain unresolved.

## Proposed listing text

**Name:** Meadowlark Ranch

**Subtitle:** Ride, care, and explore

**Promotional text:** Meet your horse, find your pace, and follow the next trail. Care for your horses, explore the ranch, and take on riding events in a world of your own.

**Description:**

Welcome to Meadowlark Ranch. Saddle up, explore the countryside, and build a life with your horses.

Choose your pace from walk through gallop, guide your horse around the ranch, and practice your jumps. Follow quests, enter riding events, and earn progress as you learn the trails.

Spend time in the stable, care for your horses, and develop their skills. Save your ranch on your device and return for another ride.

This edition is designed for offline single-player play. Its local progress stays on the device; cloud synchronization and online clubs are not included.

**Candidate keywords:** horses, riding, ranch, stable, equestrian, jumping, exploration

**Category proposal:** Games. Choose the final subcategories after reviewing the release build and App Store Connect's current options.

Do not include Star Equestrian's name, branding, screenshots, artwork, or claims of equivalence in the listing. Meadowlark needs its own verified screenshots and identity. Do not claim a frame rate, minimum-device performance, an assigned age rating, full offline reliability, or cleared distribution rights without the corresponding evidence.
