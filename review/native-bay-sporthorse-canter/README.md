# Private Bay Sporthorse two-lead Canter pilot

This is a **review-only** left- and right-lead Canter for the [prepared Bay Sporthorse](../native-breed-targets/README.md). It is not registered in the game and has no mounted, gallop, or jump approval. The WildMesh-derived source retains its [CC BY-NC 4.0 provenance](../../assets/models/horse-imports/wildmesh-white-western/provenance.json); no model was downloaded.

The [candidate](model.glb) SHA-256 is `dde4b71c76c7dbaf80c8d5ca9b06f7cbffaad85d500672d5ae4e5bbf73949e03`, built on immutable [Bay Sporthorse rest](../native-breed-targets/bay-sporthorse/rest.glb) SHA-256 `8f7f83e9669dc063dcb38455f8658369774ec0ce9b9eb81c2e7742e7d013d6a8`. The builder rederives all four sole/toe/heel masks, native leg pivots, and skinned contact goals on that target. It does not copy White or Bay angle curves. The 0.64-second three-beat cycle has 40% stance, 0.227996 m half-stroke, 0.1368/0.1277 m fore/hind lift, and nominal **1.78122 m/s** travel. Two independently solved 82-channel clips are appended, preserving the target's five meshes, 677 joints, bind matrices, maps, original Western tack, and source binary prefix.

The [build summary](build-summary.json), [actual float32 key-step audit](step-audit.json), [browser QA](qa-summary.json), and [preservation audit](preservation.json) are saved alongside [left side](left-side-250.png) and [right quarter](right-quarter-625.png) stills and the [two-lead viewer](audit-viewer.html). Browser QA sampled 128 phases per lead with 677 finite joint transforms, no page errors, **15 flight phases per lead**, no body penetration, and all planted whole-hoof minima between **0.798 and 4.024 mm** above the rest floor. At nominal travel, the maximum sampled planted regional fore/aft drift was **0.551 mm**. Independently solved loop closures are below 0.000011°; largest actual keyed limb steps are **4.780° and 4.775° per 5 ms** for left and right leads.

This is a promising contact and continuity pilot, not proof of polished full-speed movement. The right-lead offline *swing* target misses by up to **6.20 mm**, though its stance error is at most 1.40 mm and the browser contact scan passes. Many bounded solutions touch angle caps (98 left-lead and 104 right-lead per-joint frame hits), and nearly 4.8° per 5 ms remains brisk. Lateral torso roll is held at zero for stable plants, limiting sway. Mounted fit, lead changes, transitions, turning, and gameplay speed control require separate tests before integration.

Reproduce from the repository root with `python3 review/native-bay-sporthorse-canter/build.py`, then `python3 review/native-bay-sporthorse-canter/stage.py` to copy the model and derive the target foot masks. Run the local [joint](audit-steps.py) and [preservation](audit-preservation.py) audits, serve the repository, run `QA_PORT=<port> node review/native-bay-sporthorse-canter/qa.cjs`, then `python3 review/native-bay-sporthorse-canter/summarize.py`. Detailed solver and browser rows stay in ignored `output/native-bay-sporthorse-canter/`.
