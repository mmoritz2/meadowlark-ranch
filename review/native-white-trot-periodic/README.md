# Private periodic White Trot repair

This is a **review-only** repair of the realistic White Western horse's Trot. The earlier [fast-gait study](../native-white-fast-continuous/) reduced abrupt limb steps but left a 1.05669° difference between the independently solved end of the cycle and its start. It remains diagnostic. This new [Trot GLB](trot.glb), SHA-256 `83a424e5b3b10a59a146452037ac3cf06c69a12dfe66edfe6a841e09a66f0d74`, adds eight full warm-up cycles before recording; phase 1 is still solved independently, never copied. Its actual uncopied closure is 0.00000150°.

The [builder](build.py) retains the 0.72 s period, 44% diagonal stance, 0.22 m half-stroke, and original 14/12 cm fore/hind peak lift. Swing height has zero endpoint velocity, toe/sole/heel targets blend, and previous-angle penalties are bounded at 8 fore and 6 hind. The [actual float32 joint audit](trot-step-audit.json) measures a maximum 4.735° limb step per 5.625 ms, no interval over 5°, a maximum 2.50° change between consecutive joint-step vectors, and a 0.425° equivalent seam velocity difference.

The [128-phase browser QA](qa-summary.json) reports 677 finite joints, no page errors, 14 suspension phases, and all stance whole-hoof minima from −0.85 to +4.94 mm relative to the original rest floor. The [preservation audit](preservation.json) confirms the original White artwork, Western tack, five meshes, 677-joint skin, binds, materials, source binary prefix, and creator Idle/Walk curves are unchanged. Source WildMesh artwork remains CC BY-NC 4.0. Review [side](side-125.png) and [quarter](quarter-375.png) stills or open the [viewer](viewer.html) with `?gait=trot` through a local HTTP server.

This establishes numerical continuity and contact at the tested speed, not polished full-speed naturalism or mounted rider fit. The offline swing-height target misses by up to 6.41 mm; stance contacts pass. Angle caps remain active. Independent dense browser and 1× visual review should precede any combine or release. Nothing in production was edited.

Regenerate with `python3 review/native-white-trot-periodic/build.py`, copy ignored `output/native-white-trot-periodic/trot.glb` to this folder, then run the local [joint](audit-steps.py), [preservation](audit-preservation.py), and [browser](qa.cjs) audits; [summarize.py](summarize.py) condenses the results. Browser QA uses `QA_GAIT=trot QA_PORT=<port>`.
