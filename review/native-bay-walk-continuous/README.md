# Private Bay Walk continuity study

This is a **review-only** slow Walk for the realistic Bay Western horse. It is not connected to the game and is not approved as polished or mounted motion. The source artwork is WildMesh 3D's [realistic horse demo](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-realistic-3d-model-demo-free-65d6a70a6721495f938c93e80a5998e4) under [CC BY-NC 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-nc/4.0/); the derivative retains that restriction. No new model was downloaded.

The candidate [model.glb](model.glb) has SHA-256 `a9e308bdf63986405c8a93cc097dcb55c92bfc0c9075dfc4150c8c9dea729311`. Its immutable [Bay rest input](../native-bay-rollover/rest.glb) has SHA-256 `6110fb3bcfe6ba6660c9c05d67269bc800d19706fab54db29633003d87f0a6a3`. The [builder](build.py) solves the four legs against that Bay's native pivots, real skinned hoof surfaces, and original asymmetric stance. It does not transfer White joint angles. Original nodes, 677-joint skin, five meshes, inverse binds, materials, textures, Western tack, standing pose, and source binary prefix are preserved; one `Target Native Walk Rollover` clip is appended. The nominal Walk is 1.12 seconds per four-beat cycle at about 0.467 m/s, with 65% stance and heel/sole/toe rollover. The smooth swing lift, upper body, mane, and tail retain motion.

The builder uses a bounded previous-angle solution, blended contact anchors, a 3.0 forelimb and 2.0 hindlimb temporal penalty, six warm-up cycles, and an independently solved final pose. It does not copy phase zero onto phase one to hide a seam. The actual uncopied closure is 0.0000234°, and the maximum actual float32 limb key step is 4.971° in 8.75 ms at the right lower foreleg. This is much lower than the original branch-switching trials, though 568°/s at that joint is still brisk.

The [build summary](build-summary.json), [actual joint-step audit](step-audit.json), [browser QA](qa-summary.json), and [preservation audit](preservation.json) provide measured evidence. In 256 browser-sampled phases, all 677 transforms were finite with no page errors. Every phase had two or three explicit supports and no suspension. Planted whole-hoof minima stayed 0.688–1.364 mm above the fixed rest floor. At nominal travel speed, the maximum fore/aft drift of the planted heel, sole, or toe region was 0.132 mm. Body and head moved vertically by 16.3 and 33.3 mm respectively, without the original low crouch. [Side stance](side-stance.png), [side swing](side-swing.png), [quarter stance](quarter-stance.png), and [quarter swing](quarter-swing.png) stills show the pose and intact Western tack. Open the [viewer](review.html) through a local HTTP server for 1× playback.

The offline solver misses one swing-height target by up to 5.78 mm, so `authoredGridPass` remains false even though stance and browser contact checks pass. Foreleg angle caps are hit in 18 left and 17 right frames of the 128-frame authored cycle. The screenshots and sampled measurements support a coherent slow four-beat Walk, but they do not certify natural motion to a human viewer at full speed, rider contact, transitions, turning, or a faster gait. Those require separate review before game integration.

To reproduce from the repository root, run `python3 review/native-bay-walk-continuous/build.py`, `python3 review/native-bay-walk-continuous/audit-steps.py`, and `python3 review/native-bay-walk-continuous/audit-preservation.py`. The builder's detailed frame trace and model go to ignored `output/native-bay-walk-continuous/`. Run a local HTTP server, then `QA_PORT=<port> node review/native-bay-walk-continuous/qa.cjs` for the actual GLTF phase scan and screenshots. Finally run `python3 review/native-bay-walk-continuous/summarize.py` to pin the output model and compact evidence here.
