# Private Bay Canter continuity study

This is a **review-only** two-lead Canter on the original realistic Bay with Western tack. The source artwork remains WildMesh CC BY-NC 4.0; this study does not change its license. It is not wired into the game or approved as polished motion. The earlier [Bay Canter trial](../native-bay-canter/) remains diagnostic: its actual float32 curves jump 12.89° and 15.24° at the lower limbs in a 5 ms key interval. The new candidate is [model.glb](model.glb), SHA-256 `ce92a044fbc6e193dd7c3d830ebba555c29ea7695830f7b78495a242f73702b4`.

The builder starts from immutable Bay [rest.glb](../native-bay-rollover/rest.glb), SHA-256 `6110fb3bcfe6ba6660c9c05d67269bc800d19706fab54db29633003d87f0a6a3`. It solves each hoof against the Bay's actual skin, native pivots, fixed rest floor, and `_l` left-side anatomy. It does not transplant White joint rotations. The previous bounded leg solution warms the next frame; a continuity residual discourages branch changes. Swing toe/sole/heel targets blend, and hoof lift eases in over 12% of swing. Two full warm-up cycles precede each recorded 128-step cycle. Phase 1 is solved independently, never copied from phase 0. The 0.64 s period, 40% stance, 0.21242 m half-stroke, and 1.65954 m/s nominal travel match the original Bay Canter pilot.

Evidence: [build summary](build-summary.json), [actual joint-step audit](joint-step-audit.json), [browser contact/flight summary](qa-summary.json), [source preservation audit](preservation.json), plus [left-side still](left-side-250.png) and [right-quarter still](right-quarter-625.png). Browser QA sampled 128 phases per lead in Three.js: all 677 transforms finite, no browser errors, 15 unsupported flight phases per lead, all four stance whole-hoof minima within 4.71 mm of the rest floor, no body penetration. At nominal travel speed, the largest sampled fore/aft regional plant drift is 0.69 mm. The maximum actual limb key step is 4.961° left and 4.795° right over 5 ms; independently solved closure is below 0.0002°. Original 677 nodes, 5 meshes, skin weights, binds, materials, maps, and source binary prefix are preserved.

Limits matter. The offline `authoredGridPass` is false because a hind hoof misses its *swing* height target by 5.2–6.8 mm near landing; stance targets stay within 3.71 mm. Many limb solutions touch angle caps (91–92 whole-limb bound hits per lead over 128 frames), and the near-5° step threshold still represents brisk joint motion. Screenshots show a coherent three-beat sequence and some torso, neck, mane, and tail motion, but static frames cannot certify natural full-speed rhythm, rider fit, or gameplay transitions. Independent 256-phase and 1× visual review should precede any combine or release.

Reproduce from the repository root with `python3 review/native-bay-canter-smooth/build.py`, then `python3 review/native-bay-canter-smooth/audit-steps.py` and `python3 review/native-bay-canter-smooth/audit-preservation.py`. The build writes its model and detailed grid report to ignored `output/native-bay-canter-smooth/`; copy that model to this review folder before the browser audit. Serve the repository over local HTTP, then run `QA_PORT=<port> node review/native-bay-canter-smooth/qa.cjs` and `python3 review/native-bay-canter-smooth/summarize.py`. The QA script writes raw 128-phase measurements and extra screenshots to ignored output. The viewer is [audit-viewer.html](audit-viewer.html).
