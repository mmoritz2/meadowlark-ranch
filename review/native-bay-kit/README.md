# Bay Western horse: native gait kit

The original 677-joint Bay standing target, five skins, materials, groom and Western tack are preserved. This combines a gentler four-beat Walk, a separately solved diagonal Trot and both Canter leads. The game offers it as a free native model choice; actual travel stays matched to its shorter stride.

Nominal speeds are 0.466861 m/s Walk, 1.180121 m/s Trot and 1.659545 m/s Canter. Maximum keyed limb steps are 4.971°/8.75 ms Walk, 4.515°/5.625 ms Trot and 4.961°/4.795° per 5 ms Canter. Walk/Trot have periodic cubic tracks after independently verified loop recurrence. Their fixed-source contact scans preserve all 677 finite joints, upright standing geometry, heel/sole/toe plant travel and tack. Trot has low suspension clearance and regular body rhythm; limb limit plateaus remain. This is an incremental improvement, not polished naturalism, full-roster, gallop or jump approval. Mounted and runtime checks are separate from asset scans.
