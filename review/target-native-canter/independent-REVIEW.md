# Independent native Canter pilot audit

**Verdict: pass as a narrow private three-beat, two-lead white-horse study.** Both leads have the correct asymmetric sequence and a real airborne interval, anatomical limb folding, toe rollover, single-cycle axial response, moving groom and attached Western tack. No gross crouch, joint inversion or skin collapse appears in the full-cycle side/quarter sequence. It remains a slow, short-stride pilot with cap-constrained recovery and highly periodic head/body response; this is not approval of final naturalism, measured collected/working Canter, all breeds, faster gaits or rider fit.

## Ground travel and limb shape

The ±.25-m stance stroke over .256 s implies **1.953125 m/s**, ~**7.03 km/h**, and **1.25 m nominal actor travel per cycle**. This does not reproduce faster measured Canter stride lengths. Travel-compensated fixed contact trajectories use `XYZ_world = XYZ_inplace + [0,0,speed × duration × unwrapped limb phase]`. Within heel/flat/toe regimes, independent XZ range reaches **2.509 mm left / 2.530 mm right**, mainly late toe roll. This is larger than the author's key-grid anchor fit and is retained as the independent result. X ranges remain <0.0004 mm. Regional centroid Y ranges reach 3.77 mm; centroid height is not minimum contact height. These fixed centroid proxies are not individual physical contact points, and heel travel after heel lift is not planted-heel sliding. Runtime travel must match the nominal speed.

The native default posture is not symmetric: fore rest-Z sole centroids differ by ~167.5 mm between sides and hind centroids by ~206.5 mm. The pilot mirrors footfall roles and small role offsets while retaining those original per-hoof rest placements; it does not rebase the rig to a symmetric gait stance. Absolute reach/placement therefore differs between leads, and equivalent left/right natural mechanics are not established. Lead identity here is the touchdown order.

Actual swing geometry distinguishes the leading and trailing forelimbs: left lead **FL 79.63° / FR 67.45°**, right **FL 67.90° / FR 78.08°**. Hind hock maxima are **HL 83.89° / HR 86.84°** on both leads. Fore `hand_*` flexion is rearward and hind `foot_*` flexion has the opposite signed sagittal direction. Bone-center geometric angles are not clinical three-dimensional joint ROM.

The main fore bound counts concern **clavicle −18°**, held 190–245 ms across the different roles. Leading carpus **+70°** plateaus are brief: **40 ms left / 65 ms right**; trailing carpus does not reach that cap. Hind hock **−50°** plateaus last **30–110 ms**; HR **+20°** reaches its bound briefly near stance/landing. Those retained constraints can make recovery and shoulder participation mechanical. First/last pivot orientation seams are <0.000006°, hoof centroid seams <0.00004 mm. Adjacent pivot steps peak at 2.41° per 2.5 ms; pose closure does not certify smooth acceleration.

## Axial response, groom and tack

Both leads intentionally share the same axial waveform: one Canter pulse per stride. Pelvis Y range is **36.0 mm**, minimum phase .5 during overlapping/leading-fore support, maximum 0 near wrap/hind touchdown. Fixed upper-trunk marker **1998** moves **54.39 mm Y** (minimum .352, maximum .852); head pivot moves **124.42 mm Y** and ~70 mm Z (minimum .266, maximum .773). Head relative to upper trunk moves **81.75 mm Y**. The neck/head visibly protract and retract independently of the torso, but this strong periodic response remains authored motion rather than a validated natural performance. Standing body height is retained. Marker **1931 is neck crest**, not withers; 1998 is an upper-trunk probe, not a certified withers landmark. Their vectors are copied before hoof loops.

Weighted mane controls move **5.2–5.4°** locally, tail root ~**9.5°**, distal tail controls **7.2–7.5°**; distal tail travels ~**144–150 mm Z**. Groom no longer stays frozen, though it remains a restrained periodic layer rather than physical high-speed hair simulation. Saddle, stirrups, breastplate and reins stay attached without gross detachment in the inspected sequence. Rider fit was not tested.
