The HDRI is used for the visible daylight sky. Existing procedural PMREM maps
continue to provide environment lighting. The measured sun direction is
aligned by azimuth and a horizon-preserving elevation remap; the baked sun
is masked so the game's existing sun remains in use. Dawn, dusk, night and
rain retain the existing sky treatment.

RGBELoader.js is the official Three.js r160 loader, covered by the existing
Three.js MIT license in assets/vendor/three/LICENSE.
