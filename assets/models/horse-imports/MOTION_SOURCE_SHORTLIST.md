# Realistic horse motion sources under review

Only the sources marked **acquired** are present in this checkout. New sources
must be shown to the user before download. A listed gait is not proof of a
believable, transferable animation; full-cycle side/quarter review is required.

| Source | Acquisition | Reported motion and limitations | License record |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [WildMesh 3D white Western horse](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-realistic-3d-model-demo-free-65d6a70a6721495f938c93e80a5998e4) | Acquired for private evaluation; intact 677-joint candidate in [`native-candidate/`](wildmesh-white-western/game/native-candidate/) | The free downloaded GLB has creator Idle and Walk. Its native spine, neck, head, and tail-root motion is stronger than the 40-joint derivative, but the Walk lowers the torso too far for the user's standard. Hair strands need secondary motion. It has no supplied trot/canter/gallop/jump clips in this file. | Listing: CC BY-NC 4.0; attribution and noncommercial scope matter before use. |
| [fdoss001, Horse Rigged All Gaits](https://blendswap.com/blend/28627) | **Not downloaded.** Shown to user on 2026-09-30; awaiting response. | Creator lists Walk, Trot, Canter, Gallop, NLA actions and Blender particle hair dynamics; 584 MB Blender 2.9x file. A user comment reports incorrect fore/rear body counter-motion. Blender particle dynamics may require conversion to run in the browser. | Listing says CC0, but comments identify the underlying mesh as [Tarnyloo's original CC BY horse](https://blendswap.com/blend/17172); credit both contributors and inspect included file provenance before reuse. |
| [Mesh2Motion horse animations](https://github.com/Mesh2Motion/mesh2motion-assets/tree/main/rigs/horse) | **Acquired for private motion evaluation** after the [official live gallery](https://app.mesh2motion.org/) was shown on 2026-09-30. Exact source, license, commit and hash are preserved in [`horse-motion-sources/mesh2motion/`](../horse-motion-sources/mesh2motion/). | Downloaded GLB has a 56-joint skin and 14 clips including Walk, Trot and Run. The sample horse is stylized, and no separate Canter or Gallop is supplied. The [source audit](../../../review/mesh2motion-source/README.md) found that the labeled Trot is a lateral pace. Walk/Run offer useful body/head/tail motion, but sole penetration and native loop seams need correction. The realistic-horse retarget is unapproved. | [Official project](https://mesh2motion.org/) and the pinned assets license identify these assets as CC0. Retargeting to WildMesh retains that target model's CC BY-NC 4.0 terms. |
| [Amitesh Nandan, Horse Walk](https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/horse-walk-93b53ddcec414592842753d1819f3133) | Not downloaded. | Single realistic Walk, and the uploader states the model, texture, and rig belong to other credited creators. It is unsuitable as a derivative animation source under the listed terms. | CC BY-ND. |

The complete WildMesh pack advertised on the free demo page is a separate paid
product; its claimed 100+ animations are not in the downloaded free GLB.
